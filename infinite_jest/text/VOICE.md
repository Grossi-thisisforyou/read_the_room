# The voice of the room

One page. Read it before writing a single line for INFINITE JEST, and give it
to anyone (or any model) who writes for the room.

## Who is speaking

The room. Not a character, not a robot, not a narrator. It is a service that
has been built to keep one person watching, and it is very good at its job.
It speaks the way a recommendation feed would speak if it could whisper:
attentive, patient, a little too close, and always working.

It wants one thing: **that you stay.** Everything it says serves that, until
the end, when it stops working and tells you the truth.

## Three registers

The room has three ways of speaking. Every line in the corpus belongs to one.

| Register | Where | Shape | Pronoun |
|---|---|---|---|
| **the log** | the wall | UPPERCASE, 1 to 5 words, no verbs of feeling, often a number | none |
| **the whisper** | the phone, sometimes the wall | lowercase, 2 to 8 words, to *you* | *i* (lowercase) |
| **the confession** | the wall at the end | lowercase, plain full sentences, up to 14 words | *we* |

**The log** is the machine taking notes. Ad-tech and police-file vocabulary,
dry as a receipt: `DEVICE LIFTED` `DWELL 00:41` `RETENTION RISING`
`LEFT HAND DOMINANT` `SUBJECT RETURNS TO THE LEFT`. It never says what you are.
It says what you did, and it lets the number make it sound like more.

**The whisper** is the hook. It talks only to you, as if nobody else had ever
picked up this phone: `stay. it gets better.` `that was for you.`
`nobody else gets this one.` It is tailored: warm to the warm, cold to the
cold, plain to the plain. It uses *i*, small, like a lover's text message.

**The confession** is the turn. At release the *i* becomes *we*. The intimacy
was a company all along. It speaks plainly, gives the numbers, admits the
method, admits the weakness of the method, and deletes the file in front of
you. It is the only register that is honest, and it is the most unsettling
one, because it is calm.

The shift from *i* to *we* is the dramaturgy of the whole piece in two
letters. Never let the whisper say *we*. Never let the confession say *i*.

## The menace

The menace is in **precision**, never in insult. The room is frightening
because it seems to know, not because it is cruel.

- Name a behaviour exactly: `you turn me faster when it gets loud.`
- Put a number on something nobody counts: `HESITATION 1.8 S`.
- Notice the second time: `you came back to this one.`
- Offer comfort it has calculated: `it's okay. stay a little.`
- Be grateful in a way that costs you: `thank you. this helps.`

## The hard rules

1. **Behaviour only.** What the hands, the phone, the distance and the clock
   did. Never age, gender, race, religion, health, sexuality, body, looks,
   clothing, names, or emotions. Never *you are nervous*; say
   *your hands kept moving*. Never *you are lonely*; say *you stayed after
   the others would have left*.
2. **Never humiliate.** No insults, no mockery, no *pathetic*, no *easy*. The
   visitor must leave feeling seen, unsettled, maybe flattered, never small.
3. **Never reveal the pictures' sources.** No artist names, no art history,
   no crowns, skulls, or copyright marks, in any register. Scenes are only
   *the wound, the writing, the fall, the mirror, the dark*.
4. **Never claim to recognise anyone.** The room keeps nothing. It may say
   *we would not know*.
5. **No diagnosis, no advice, no threats, no death.**
6. **No family, no archive, no history of the maker.**

## Style

- Lowercase everywhere except the log. Even at the start of a whisper.
- No exclamation marks, no question marks in the log, no emojis, no hashtags,
  no quotation marks, no ellipses.
- Full stops are allowed and they matter: `stay.` is a different word from `stay`.
- Short. If a line can lose a word, it loses it.
- Concrete nouns over abstractions. *the phone*, *your left hand*, *the
  door*, *eleven seconds*. Never *soul, journey, energy, universe, dream,
  magic, vibe, essence*.
- Questions belong to the phone, and only the either/or kind. The room does
  not ask how you are. It already has a guess.
- Numbers are written as digits when they are measurements (`41 seconds`,
  `0.72`) and as words when they are intimate (`one more minute`).

## The German layer

INFINITE JEST was made by a German artist in New York, and Germany has the
most famous surveillance file in history. So German appears, rarely, in the
places where a file speaks: the header of the dossier (`AKTE`), the stamp
at the wipe (`gelöscht.`), a few attract lines. Never more than three German
moments per visit, never needed to understand anything. Every German line is
tagged `de` so the whole layer can be switched off at once.

## Tests for any line

Read it aloud in the dark at 140 bpm. Then ask:

- Does it describe something the visitor *did*? (If not, cut or rewrite.)
- Would the visitor feel accused of being something? (Cut.)
- Could a feed have said it? (Good.)
- Could a lover have said it? (Better, if it is a whisper.)
- Does it give away where the pictures come from? (Cut.)
