INFINITE JEST · "Stone" · Raspberry Pi 5 case
Three printed parts: one tray, two shells that click onto it.

PARTS
  stone_tray.stl    122 x 101 x 13 mm   bottom face down, no supports
  stone_work.stl    121 x 100 x 40 mm   rim down, supports inside (see below)
  stone_travel.stl  125 x 102 x 40 mm   rim down, supports inside
  stone.scad        OpenSCAD source (change `seed` for a differently cut stone)
The top of the stone is uneven on purpose, so the shells print rim down with
TREE SUPPORTS, BUILD PLATE ONLY: they stand on the bed inside the shell and
touch only the inside of the crown, so no outside surface is marked.
Bed at least 130 x 110 mm.

MARKS
  J R A is pressed into the top of each shell and raised on the inside wall at
  the microSD end of each shell. Under the tray: J R A, INFINITE JEST, "Raspberry Pi 5 16 GB",
  "return to Jasper Engelhardt", "jasperengelhardt.com". The small letters are
  pressed 0.6 mm into the tray face on the bed, so print the first layer slowly.

MATERIAL AND SETTINGS
  PETG or ASA, matte black (not PLA: it softens next to a warm Pi)
  0.12 mm layers for the best facets, 4 walls (perimeters), 20 % gyroid infill
  Shells: 4 walls makes the 2.2 mm shell almost solid, which is what the click needs

THE CLICK
  The tray has a rim with a groove; each shell has small ridges inside its edge
  that snap into it. Push the shell straight down until it clicks, pull straight
  up to open.
  PLEASE TEST FIRST: print the tray and the travel shell and try the click before
  printing the work shell. Too tight: set clr = 0.35 (or bump_d = 0.4) in
  stone.scad and export again. Too loose: bump_d = 0.7.

BAND CROSS
  Two fine channels (3.5 mm wide, 0.9 mm deep) run round the stone, one the long
  way and one across; they cross on the crown and under the tray. Two hair ties
  or rubber bands in them keep the shell shut in a bag. Change band_w / band_d in stone.scad to suit the band.

HARDWARE
  4x M2.5 x 6 mm self-tapping screws (Pi to the tray standoffs)
    or brass heat-set inserts: set threads = "insert" in stone.scad
  2 hair ties or rubber bands, about 3 mm thick, for the bag
  4x 8 mm self-adhesive rubber bumpers (seats are marked under the tray)
  Official Raspberry Pi 5 Active Cooler (needed) and 27 W USB-C supply

OPENINGS (work shell)
  port end      4x USB, Ethernet
  long side     USB-C power, micro-HDMI 0 and 1
  microSD end   power button, status LEDs
  tray          microSD card slot under the board; air slots in the floor
  roof + GPIO side: ventilation slits over the fan
The travel shell is closed and protects the ports in a bag.

Port positions come from the Pi 5 drawings and are not yet checked on a print:
please do one quick draft of the tray + work shell and try the board in it.

Export:
  openscad -D 'part="tray"'   -o stone_tray.stl   stone.scad
  openscad -D 'part="work"'   -o stone_work.stl   stone.scad
  openscad -D 'part="travel"' -o stone_travel.stl stone.scad
